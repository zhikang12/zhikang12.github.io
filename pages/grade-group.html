Clinical Pharmacology GRADE Group

An independent academic initiative focused on methodological challenges in assessing clinical pharmacology evidence.

Methods · Certainty · Evidence

METHODOLOGICAL FOCUS

Developing methods for complex evidence.

The Group examines how GRADE methods can be applied or further developed for questions in clinical pharmacology and pharmacotherapy, including evidence beyond conventional head-to-head trials.

Areas of inquiry

  • Assessment of certainty in indirect evidence for therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM) and pharmacogenomics (PGx)
  • Causal attribution of adverse drug events and related certainty of evidence
  • Very large observed effects in small populations and how they inform certainty judgments
  • Additional methodological questions arising from clinical pharmacology research

METHODOLOGICAL PRINCIPLE

Interpret the whole body of evidence.

Clinical decisions may rely on randomized evidence, observational studies, PK/PD, PGx and other sources. Each evidence stream has strengths, limitations and questions of applicability.

ACADEMIC RELATIONSHIP

Independent methodology, complementary applications.

The Clinical Pharmacology GRADE Group concentrates on methodological development. The International Clinical Pharmacology EBM Group focuses on guidelines, application and implementation. The two initiatives retain independent academic judgment.
