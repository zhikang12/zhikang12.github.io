CAPTURE / Academic Initiatives

International Clinical Pharmacology EBM Group

An independent academic initiative advancing evidence-informed clinical guidance, implementation and international research exchange.

Guidelines · Implementation · Education

EVIDENCE INTO PRACTICE

From evidence assessment to clinical recommendations.

The Group focuses on applying research evidence and appropriate methods to important clinical pharmacology and pharmacotherapy decisions.

Areas of inquiry

  • International clinical practice guideline development and guideline methodology
  • Appropriate-use and necessity guidance for TDM and PGx testing
  • Other high-priority or emerging pharmacotherapy questions
  • Knowledge translation, implementation and education
  • International academic and research collaboration

EVIDENCE-INFORMED DECISIONS

Recommendations in context.

Evidence certainty, benefits and harms, patient preferences and implementation considerations can all shape clinical recommendations.

ACADEMIC RELATIONSHIP

Guidance informed by robust methods.

The EBM Group focuses on guideline applications and implementation, while the Clinical Pharmacology GRADE Group focuses on methodological questions. They are distinct academic initiatives, and CAPTURE does not control their recommendations.
