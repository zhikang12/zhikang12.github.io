HOW WE WORK

Scientific independence remains non-negotiable.

CAPTURE may undertake commissioned research, with transparent roles and methods appropriate to the scientific question.

Principles for responsible collaboration

  • The research question, scope, methods and deliverables should be clearly defined.
  • Authors and contributors retain appropriate scientific judgment and accountability.
  • Funding and relevant interests should be disclosed where applicable.
  • Commissioned work does not confer control over independent academic initiatives or their guideline recommendations.
