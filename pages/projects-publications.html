PRIOR SCHOLARLY COLLABORATION / 2020

Gastrointestinal bleeding prophylaxis in critically ill patients

A linked clinical practice guideline and network meta-analysis explored the benefits and harms of preventive medications in critical care; a subsequent updated review incorporated further randomized evidence.

Related publications (3)

  1. Gastrointestinal bleeding prophylaxis for critically ill patients: a clinical practice guideline ↗BMJ · 2020 · Guideline · DOI: 10.1136/bmj.l6722
  2. Efficacy and safety of gastrointestinal bleeding prophylaxis in critically ill patients: systematic review and network meta-analysis ↗BMJ · 2020 · Systematic review · DOI: 10.1136/bmj.l6744
  3. Efficacy and safety of gastrointestinal bleeding prophylaxis in critically ill patients: an updated systematic review and network meta-analysis of randomized trials ↗Intensive Care Medicine · 2020 · Updated review · DOI: 10.1007/s00134-020-06209-w

PRIOR SCHOLARLY COLLABORATION / 2020

Evidence-informed treatment guidance for COVID-19

An evidence-based guideline was developed alongside three systematic reviews addressing corticosteroids, antiviral medications and convalescent plasma. The reviews drew on direct and indirect evidence available during the early pandemic.

Related publications (4)

  1. Treatment of patients with nonsevere and severe coronavirus disease 2019: an evidence-based guideline ↗CMAJ · 2020 · Guideline · DOI: 10.1503/cmaj.200648
  2. Efficacy and safety of corticosteroids in COVID-19 based on evidence for COVID-19, other coronavirus infections, influenza, community-acquired pneumonia and acute respiratory distress syndrome: a systematic review and meta-analysis ↗CMAJ · 2020 · Systematic review · DOI: 10.1503/cmaj.200645
  3. Efficacy and safety of antiviral treatment for COVID-19 from evidence in studies of SARS-CoV-2 and other acute viral infections: a systematic review and meta-analysis ↗CMAJ · 2020 · Systematic review · DOI: 10.1503/cmaj.200647
  4. Efficacy and safety of convalescent plasma for severe COVID-19 based on evidence in other severe respiratory viral infections: a systematic review and meta-analysis ↗CMAJ · 2020 · Systematic review · DOI: 10.1503/cmaj.200642

PRIOR SCHOLARLY COLLABORATION / 2022–2023

Endovascular thrombectomy and intravenous alteplase in acute ischemic stroke

An international guideline addressing thrombectomy alone versus thrombectomy combined with alteplase was informed by a systematic review of randomized trials and complemented by a Canada–China cost-effectiveness analysis.

Related publications (3)

  1. Endovascular thrombectomy and intravenous alteplase in patients with acute ischemic stroke due to large vessel occlusion: a clinical practice guideline ↗Journal of Evidence-Based Medicine · 2022 · Guideline · DOI: 10.1111/jebm.12493
  2. Endovascular thrombectomy with or without intravenous alteplase for acute ischemic stroke due to large vessel occlusion: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized trials ↗Stroke & Vascular Neurology · 2022 · Systematic review · DOI: 10.1136/svn-2022-001547
  3. Cost-effectiveness of endovascular thrombectomy with alteplase versus endovascular thrombectomy alone for acute ischemic stroke secondary to large vessel occlusion ↗CMAJ Open · 2023 · Economic evaluation · DOI: 10.9778/cmajo.20220096

ONGOING INDEPENDENT ACADEMIC RESEARCH

GRADE certainty assessment of indirect evidence for TDM and PGx

The Clinical Pharmacology GRADE Group is developing methodological approaches for assessing certainty in indirect evidence informing therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM) and pharmacogenomics (PGx) decisions.

Methodological focus

  • Distinguishing direct evidence from different forms of indirect evidence.
  • Appraising links between PK/PD, drug exposure, genomic variation and patient-important outcomes.
  • Communicating certainty, applicability and remaining uncertainty transparently.
