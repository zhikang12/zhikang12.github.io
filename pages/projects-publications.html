RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

Where evidence and decision-making meet.

These topics describe CAPTURE’s areas of research interest; individual studies and outputs are attributed to their responsible investigators and organizations.

01 / METHODS

Evidence certainty & indirect evidence

Methods for assessing the trustworthiness, applicability and limitations of evidence informing pharmacotherapy decisions.

02 / THERAPEUTICS

TDM, PGx & medication safety

Clinical evidence on testing, monitoring, exposure–response relationships and benefits and harms of medicines.

03 / TRANSLATION

Guideline methods & knowledge translation

Connecting evidence assessment with transparent recommendations and decision-relevant research priorities.

PROJECTS & PUBLICATIONS

A transparent record of research.

Individual projects and publications will be listed with accurate titles, author attribution, identifiers and relevant disclosures when their details are verified and cleared for public release.

Research projects

Project descriptions will identify the actual responsible investigators, organizations and status, rather than treating independent academic initiatives as CAPTURE projects.

Selected publications

Publications will be linked to their verifiable journal records or persistent identifiers. Scholarly contributions will be attributed to the relevant authors and initiatives.
