Evidence. Methods. Therapeutics.

PRECISION THERAPEUTICS

Clinical pharmacology as a bridge between mechanisms and outcomes.

Medicines are used in patients, not in isolation. Dose, exposure, response, genetics and clinical context can each matter.

TDM

Therapeutic drug monitoring

Evidence for appropriate monitoring, concentration-guided dosing and clinically meaningful outcomes.

PGx

Pharmacogenomics

Evidence for the value and appropriate use of genetic testing to inform drug therapy.

PK / PD

Pharmacokinetics & pharmacodynamics

Interpreting exposure–response evidence and its connection to therapeutic benefits and harms.

SAFETY

Drug safety & adverse reactions

Assessment of adverse drug events, causal attribution and the certainty of benefit–harm evidence.

PHARMACOTHERAPY

The broader question is better use of medicines.

Our interests extend beyond tests and monitoring to clinically important pharmacotherapy decisions.

Decisions across the care pathway

Questions may involve who should receive a treatment, how treatment should be selected or adjusted, how benefits and harms should be weighed, and when additional testing or monitoring is justified.

We are particularly interested in integrating randomized evidence with observational, PK/PD, PGx and real-world research where appropriate.

A CORE PRINCIPLE

Clinical outcomes first.

Biological plausibility and pharmacological measurements must be linked carefully to outcomes important to patients.
