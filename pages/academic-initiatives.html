CAPTURE / Academic Initiatives

Two independent initiatives. Two complementary purposes.

INDEPENDENT ACADEMIC INITIATIVES

From evaluating evidence to informing practice.

These initiatives are presented together for their scientific relevance. Each retains responsibility for its own academic activities, decisions and outputs.

01 METHODOLOGY

CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY GRADE GROUP

Developing methods for complex evidence.

The Clinical Pharmacology GRADE Group addresses challenges in applying GRADE approaches to evidence about medicines, pharmacotherapy and testing.

  • Certainty of indirect evidence for therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM) and pharmacogenomics (PGx)
  • Causal attribution and certainty of evidence for adverse drug events
  • Assessment of very large effects observed in small populations
  • Emerging methodological questions in clinical pharmacology

02 GUIDELINES & PRACTICE

INTERNATIONAL CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY EBM GROUP

Translating evidence into recommendations.

The International Clinical Pharmacology EBM Group focuses on evidence-informed clinical guidance, its implementation and international scholarly exchange.

  • International clinical practice guidelines and guideline methodology
  • Appropriate use and necessity of TDM and PGx
  • High-priority pharmacotherapy questions and emerging interventions
  • Knowledge translation, implementation and education
  • International academic collaboration

ACADEMIC INDEPENDENCE

Independent scientific judgment.

CAPTURE owns this website, but does not own or control either academic initiative. Commissioned CAPTURE projects do not automatically involve the Groups or their members, and financial support cannot determine evidence judgments or guideline recommendations.

Authorship, organizational responsibility, funding and relevant interests should be accurately disclosed for each scholarly output.
