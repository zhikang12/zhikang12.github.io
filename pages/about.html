WHO WE ARE

An independent foundation with a focused scientific purpose.

CAPTURE Evidence & Clinical Pharmacology Foundation is an independent not-for-profit research foundation dedicated to strengthening the evidence used in medicines and healthcare decisions.

Our focus

CAPTURE works at the intersection of evidence-based medicine, clinical pharmacology and pharmacotherapy. We are particularly interested in questions where sound treatment and health care decisions require evidence from randomized controlled trials, observational studies, pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic research, pharmacogenomics, real-world evidence and other clinical sources.

Our scientific scope includes evidence synthesis, research methodology, study design, guideline methods and knowledge translation.

ORGANIZATIONAL IDENTITY

Independent research. International collaboration.

CAPTURE seeks to collaborate with researchers, universities, healthcare organizations and other partners around the world to advance rigorous, decision-relevant research and knowledge translation.

WHAT WE BRING

Research strengths grounded in evidence and methods.

Our approach brings together complementary research methods, clinically relevant questions and international collaboration, with scientific integrity and transparent attribution at its core.

01 / INTEGRATED METHODS

Integrated research expertise

Evidence synthesis, GRADE certainty assessment, study design, clinical pharmacology and guideline methodology, applied to questions about medicines and healthcare.

02 / COLLABORATION

International research collaboration

Connecting complementary perspectives across research disciplines and settings to address important clinical and methodological questions.

03 / APPLICATION

Evidence-to-decision approach

Moving beyond summarizing findings to consider benefits and harms, certainty of evidence and the information needed for responsible decisions.

ACADEMIC RELATIONSHIPS

Distinct identities and independent academic judgment.

The two academic initiatives featured on this website have distinct scientific purposes and retain their academic independence.

The Clinical Pharmacology GRADE Group focuses on methodological development, while the International Clinical Pharmacology EBM Group focuses on clinical guidance and implementation. CAPTURE’s commissioned work does not automatically involve either initiative.
