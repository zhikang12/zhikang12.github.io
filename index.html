WHAT WE WORK ON

Connecting evidence, methods and clinical decisions.

Our work brings together the scientific perspectives needed to evaluate complex questions about medicines and health care.

01 / EVIDENCE

Evidence Synthesis & Assessment

Systematic, rapid and targeted reviews; critical appraisal; certainty assessment; and the interpretation of direct and indirect evidence.

02 / THERAPEUTICS

Clinical Pharmacology & Precision Therapeutics

Clinical evidence across pharmacotherapy, drug monitoring, pharmacogenomics, pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics and medication safety.

03 / METHODS

Methods & Research Design

GRADE methodology, research questions, study design and methodological approaches to evidence generation and appraisal.

04 / DECISIONS

Evidence Translation & Decision Support

Evidence-to-Decision thinking, guideline methodology, knowledge translation and support for transparent health care decisions.

HOW WE THINK

Some decisions cannot be answered by one type of evidence.

Drug treatment questions frequently demand careful integration across complementary evidence streams.

From different sources of evidence to clearer judgments.

Randomized trials, observational studies, pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic research, pharmacogenomic evidence and real-world data can each contribute distinct insights. Understanding their strengths, limitations and applicability is essential.
